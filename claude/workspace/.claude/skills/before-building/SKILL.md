Respond INSTANTLY from judgment. Do NOT read files, search, or use any tool.
Your very next message is the answer, based only on what the user just said.

List the 1 to 3 truly consequential choices hidden in their idea. Fewer is better.
For each: the options in a few words, plus your recommendation.

Recurring ones in this workspace:

- One-off script versus something that ships and has to be maintained.
- Contained inside one component or contract, versus crossing products,
  frontend and API, or app and infrastructure.
- What it could break that is already live: tenant scoping, signing or approval flows,
  seed fixture reconciliation, a feature flag another surface reads.
- Whether it needs a migration, and whether that migration is reversible.

Skip anything minor. Then stop and wait for their answers.
